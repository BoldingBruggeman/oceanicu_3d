  ## 1. Correctness bugs
  
  **B1. StatsDB inserts a duplicate row on every re-run when `depth_label` is NULL. APPLY**
  - Where: `lib/stats_db.py:68, 92-93` (schema), `301-345` (`_upsert`), `409-411`.
  - What: SQLite treats NULLs as distinct in `UNIQUE(...)`, so `ON CONFLICT` never fires for 2-D validations and top-level profile rows. I reproduced it in a scratch DB: three `record_validation` calls with the same key gave three rows, each call returned id 1 (the oldest), and `get_ranking` returned all three. Monthly stats therefore attach to the stale row and rankings mix old and new results.
  - `tests/test_stats_db.py:26` works around it ("depth_label must be non-NULL").
  - Fix: store `''` instead of NULL for `depth_label` and `obs_source` (or a unique index on `COALESCE(...)`), add a one-off dedupe migration keeping the newest `run_date`, and change `depth_label IS NULL` at line 704 accordingly.
  - Risk: medium (schema and migration on the live `statistics.db`). Tests: add a NULL-key round-trip. Size: about 40 lines plus migration.
  
  **B2. Residual parquet is overwritten once per year in wod, cruise and glodap. APPLY**
  - Where: `cli/wod_profiles.py:294`, `cli/cruise_ctd_profiles.py:289`, `cli/glodap_profiles.py:327` call `validate_profiles_against_model` inside the year loop with no `residuals_collector`; `lib/_profile_utils.py:693-708` then writes `output_dir/<obs_type>_residuals.parquet` with no year in the name.
  - Scenario: a 2010–2020 run leaves only 2020's residuals, and `mle-comparison` silently ranks experiments on the last year alone.
  - Fix: pass a collector (as `ices_profiles.py:230-276` does) and write once after the loop; move the "concat, thin, write" block into one `write_residuals(parts, cfg, path)`.
  - Risk: low. Tests: none. Size: about 30 lines.
  
  **B3. Spatial correlation map is wrong wherever obs has temporal gaps. APPLY**
  - Where: `cli/gridded_2d_validation.py:1267-1303`.
  - What: `sum_m`, `sum_m2`, `sum_o` and `sum_mo` are NaN-skipping sums over all times, but they are divided by `n_v`, the count of paired-valid times. For a cell with 365 model days and 100 obs days, `mean_m` comes out 3.65 times too large, and `std_m`/`cov`/correlation are garbage (often NaN from a negative square root). RMSE, bias and MAE are correct.
  - Fix: mask both arrays with pair validity before accumulating, and move the accumulator to `lib/statistics.py` as `spatial_statistics_chunked(model, obs, chunk=30)`. That replaces three implementations: this one, `Validation2D.compute_spatial_statistics` (`lib/validation_2d_analysis.py:378`) and `StatisticsCalculator.compute_spatial_statistics` (`lib/statistics.py:252`).
  - Risk: low; it only changes results for L3 SST or CHL-type obs. Tests: none for this path. Size: about 60 lines.
  
  **B4. `compute_all` means and standard deviations are not computed on paired samples. DISCUSS (changes published numbers)**
  - Where: `lib/statistics.py:238-241`.
  - What: `mean_model`, `mean_obs`, `std_model`, `std_obs` use each array's own NaN mask, so `mean_model − mean_obs ≠ bias`, and Taylor diagrams use an unpaired std ratio. `_prepare_obs` (`cli/gridded_2d_validation.py:139-170`) only applies an any-time coverage mask, so per-timestep gaps remain.
  - Fix: apply the `valid` mask before all moments.
  - Tests: `tests/test_statistics.py` covers `compute_all`, but not with asymmetric NaNs. Size: 10 lines.
