NSe — Future Boundary Conditions

Cross-model, cross-scenario summary of NSe’s future open-boundary conditions: delta-change temperature/salinity (CMIP6, bias-corrected against the CMEMS Northwest Shelf reanalysis) and tidal elevation/currents (TPXO9 plus the CMIP6 ensemble-mean sea-level trend). Biogeochemical boundaries are not yet included here – CMIP6 ESM biogeochemistry is considerably simpler than the CMEMS regional product, and whether a delta-change approach is even appropriate for it is still under evaluation (see the feasibility-check section below for the evidence gathered so far). ...
