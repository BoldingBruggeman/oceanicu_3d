Methodology

ICES Point Profiles

Quality-controlled hydrographic profiles from the ICES data portal (temperature, salinity, biogeochemical variables).

Argo Float Profiles

Autonomous profiling floats providing temperature and salinity profiles to 2000 m depth (Ifremer ERDDAP).
