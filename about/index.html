About

October 23, 2026 · Ocean Modeling Team

This site presents validation results and future-scenario projections for pyGETM ocean model setups developed within the OceanICU project.

Modeling areas#

  • NSe — an extended North Sea domain, covering the North Sea and its approaches down to the Baltic entrance.
  • NS — a GETM setup similar to the legacy GETM North Sea setup, updated with GEBCO 2025 bathymetry and current forcing.
  • AMM7 — a well-tested shelf-seas configuration, also run with NEMO as the model, from PML.

Each area’s own page lists its real experiments, validation results, and (where applicable) future-scenario boundary/forcing data.

Forcing and boundary data#

Historical atmospheric forcing, ocean boundaries and river discharge come from reanalysis products (ERA5, CMEMS, EMORID). Future scenarios add CMIP6 model output as the climate-change signal, either bias- corrected directly against ERA5 (atmospheric forcing) or as a delta- change applied on top of the historical reanalysis (ocean boundaries). See Data Sources for exactly what feeds each area, and Scenarios for the resulting projections.
